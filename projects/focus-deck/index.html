About Focus Deck

Your day on one screen: top priorities, upcoming events, focus timers, habit streaks and notes, with a place for every uni subject.

Everything stays on your device unless you turn on the optional weather or Reminders sync.

Features

  • Today view with priorities, calendar events, a focus ring and habit streaks
  • Tasks with natural-language quick add and optional Apple Reminders sync
  • Subjects: tasks and timers for each uni subject or project
  • Several focus timers at once, with Pomodoro cycles and a menu bar countdown
  • Habits with a 12-week heatmap, and Markdown notes

Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later

Privacy

Everything stays on your device unless you turn on the optional weather lookup or Reminders sync.

FAQ

Is Focus Deck free?

Yes. Focus Deck is completely free, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Is it safe to open an app that macOS warns me about?

The warning appears because Focus Deck isn't notarised by Apple (that needs a paid membership), not because anything is wrong. Only download it from this site or its official GitHub page, and macOS will only ask you once.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Focus Deck is built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later), so it won't run on older Intel Macs.
