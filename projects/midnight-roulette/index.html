Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. MidnightRoulette.dmg

    1. Open the download

    Double-click MidnightRoulette.dmg in your Downloads folder.

  2. Applications

    2. Drag it to Applications

    Drag MidnightRoulette onto the Applications folder.

    • Open
    • Show in Finder
    • Get Info

    3. Right-click → Open, the first time

    Then click Open again. After that, open it normally.

Why the extra step? I'm a student, so my apps aren't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account). macOS asks you to confirm once that you trust an app from the internet. The app is exactly the one built from my source, and you can check the checksum below.

  1. MidnightRoulette-Windows.zip

    1. Unzip it

    Right-click MidnightRoulette-Windows.zip → Extract All…

  2. MidnightRoulette.exe

    2. Run it

    Open the folder and double-click MidnightRoulette.exe.

  3. Windows protected your PCMore infoRun anyway

    3. More info → Run anyway

    If SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Why does Windows warn me? SmartScreen warns about apps that haven't been downloaded many times yet or aren't signed with a paid certificate. It's a one-time check.

What's new

Version 1.0.1

10 November 2026

Changed

  • The eight diamond deflectors on the ball track are larger polished brass and the ball strikes one on most spins.
  • Taller, thicker frets between the pockets, and a livelier rattle: the ball hops pocket to pocket, can be knocked back a pocket or overshoot the result and bounce back. Results are still drawn fairly when you press Spin.

    Added

    • European single-zero roulette with every standard bet: straight up, splits (including 0-1, 0-2, 0-3), streets, trios, corners, First Four, six lines, columns, dozens and the six even-money bets.
    • A smooth 3D wheel built entirely from procedural meshes and shaders, with a ball path that is planned to land in the result drawn when you press Spin. Normal (~10 s), fast (~4 s) and instant modes, plus an optional ball-following camera.
    • A vertical betting table with exact click zones for every bet, hover previews, drag-to-move stacks, undo, rebet, double and saved bet layouts ("My Bets").
