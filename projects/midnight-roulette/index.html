About Midnight Roulette

A real single-zero European wheel with a smooth 3D bowl, gold frets and a ball that rolls, drops and rattles into its pocket.

Every bet is here, including racetrack call bets, with history, statistics and original lounge music. Play money only: no purchases, ads or gambling sites.

Screenshots

Features

  • A real European wheel: single zero, 37 pockets and realistic ball physics
  • Every bet, plus racetrack call bets like Voisins du Zéro and Orphelins
  • Chips drop and stack, and winnings slide over to you
  • Fair: results come from a cryptographically seeded random number generator
  • History and statistics for the last 500 spins
  • Colour-blind markers, larger text, reduce motion and controller support

Requirements

  • Mac: Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 11 Big Sur or later
  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with Vulkan or DirectX 12 graphics

How to install

On a Mac

  1. 1. Download and open

    Download MidnightRoulette.dmg and double-click it in your Downloads folder. A window opens.

  2. 2. Drag it to Applications

    Drag Midnight Roulette onto the Applications folder. Then you can eject the disk image.

  3. 3. The first time: right-click → Open

    In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Midnight Roulette and choose Open, then click Open again. After that, open it normally.

    Why? Apple checks apps from the internet for a paid "notarisation" stamp. I'm a student, so my apps aren't notarised yet. That doesn't mean anything is wrong. macOS just asks you to confirm once that you trust where the app came from.

  4. No "Open" option? (macOS 15 Sequoia and later)

    Newer versions of macOS may only offer Done or Move to Bin. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Midnight Roulette. You only need to do this once.

On Windows

  1. 1. Download and unzip

    Download MidnightRoulette-Windows.zip, right-click it and choose Extract All….

  2. 2. Run it

    Open the folder and double-click the .exe. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Like on the Mac, this is because the game isn't signed with a paid certificate.

How updates work

Midnight Roulette checks for a new version at most once a day. When there's an update, it shows what's new and a Download update button. You can turn this off or check now in Settings → Updates.

Privacy

Everything stays on your computer. The only thing that goes online is the optional update check, which asks GitHub for the latest version number. No accounts, analytics or tracking.

FAQ

Can I win real money?

No. Midnight Roulette uses play money only. Credits have no cash value and can't be bought or withdrawn.

Is Midnight Roulette free?

Yes. Midnight Roulette is completely free, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Is it safe to open an app that macOS warns me about?

The warning appears because Midnight Roulette isn't notarised by Apple (that needs a paid membership), not because anything is wrong. Only download it from this site or its official GitHub page, and macOS will only ask you once.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Midnight Roulette is built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later), so it won't run on older Intel Macs.

  2. 1.0.0

    Added

    • European single-zero roulette with every standard bet: straight up, splits (including 0-1, 0-2, 0-3), streets, trios, corners, First Four, six lines, columns, dozens and the six even-money bets.

    • Dolly, rake and payout animations, and tasteful celebrations for straight-up, big and huge wins.
    • Results history (last 500 spins), statistics for the last 100 spins and all time, hot/cold numbers and an optional heat overlay.
    • Configurable table limits, La Partage, extra $500/$1,000 chips, casino-mode betting timer and swap-panels layout.
    • Original synthesised sound effects, lounge music and casino ambience; optional dealer voice using the computer's text-to-speech.
    • Accessibility: colour-blind markers, text size 80–150%, reduce motion, full keyboard and controller support.
    • Wellbeing reminders and a clear play-money-only notice.
    • Auto-save after every bet and spin (atomic writes) with safe recovery if the game closes mid-spin.
    • In-game update checker that looks for new releases on GitHub.
