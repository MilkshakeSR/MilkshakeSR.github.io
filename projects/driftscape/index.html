About Driftscape

Oceans, rain on glass, aurora, fireplaces and more: 38 original wallpapers drawn live with Metal, not video loops.

The sun is calculated for where you are, so sunrise, golden hour and starry nights happen when they really do. Clouds, rain and snow follow your local weather, and if you like, wallpapers breathe gently with the music your Mac is playing.

Screenshots

Features

  • 38 living wallpapers in six collections: Water, Rain & Windows, Space, Nature, City & Cosy and Abstract
  • Real sunrise, sunset and moon phase for where you are
  • Live weather: clouds, rain, snow, fog, wind and storms fade in gently
  • Optional music reactivity that never flashes
  • Studio: tweak every wallpaper with sliders, colours and palettes, and save presets
  • Schedules for dawn, day, golden hour and night, and a different wallpaper on each display
  • Export 5K stills and seamless video loops, plus a matching screen saver
  • Easy on power: pauses completely when the desktop is covered, the screen is locked or Low Power Mode is on

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later)
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later (music reactivity needs macOS 14.2 or later)

How to install

On a Mac

  1. 1. Download and open

    Download Driftscape.dmg and double-click it in your Downloads folder. A window opens.

  2. 2. Drag it to Applications

    Drag Driftscape onto the Applications folder. Then you can eject the disk image.

  3. 3. The first time: right-click → Open

    In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Driftscape and choose Open, then click Open again. After that, open it normally.

    Why? Apple checks apps from the internet for a paid "notarisation" stamp. I'm a student, so my apps aren't notarised yet. That doesn't mean anything is wrong. macOS just asks you to confirm once that you trust where the app came from.

  4. No "Open" option? (macOS 15 Sequoia and later)

    Newer versions of macOS may only offer Done or Move to Bin. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Driftscape. You only need to do this once.

How updates work

Driftscape checks for updates automatically once a day. You can also choose Check for Updates… from the menu bar icon, or turn on automatic installs in Settings → Updates.

Privacy

Your wallpapers, presets and settings stay on your Mac. There are no analytics or tracking. What leaves your Mac: your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) goes to Open-Meteo for the weather, and update checks go to GitHub. Music is analysed live on your Mac and never recorded or sent anywhere.

FAQ

Will it drain my battery?

No. Driftscape draws at 30 frames per second at 75% resolution by default, and stops completely when windows cover the desktop, the screen is locked or Low Power Mode is on. There's also an optional battery saver.

Why does it ask for my location?

So the sky matches your real sunrise, sunset and weather. Your location is rounded to about 1 km before it's used, and you can type a city instead.

Is Driftscape free?

Yes. Driftscape is completely free, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Is it safe to open an app that macOS warns me about?

The warning appears because Driftscape isn't notarised by Apple (that needs a paid membership), not because anything is wrong. Only download it from this site or its official GitHub page, and macOS will only ask you once.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Driftscape is built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later), so it won't run on older Intel Macs.
