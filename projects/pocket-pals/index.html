Meet Pocket Pals.

Little animals that live on your screen. They walk along your Dock, nap on your windows, wake up when you come back and remind you to stretch, drink water and rest your eyes.

Every pal is drawn in code and has its own personality, and clicks go straight through to your apps unless you click a pet.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. PocketPals.dmg

    1. Open the download

    Double-click PocketPals.dmg in your Downloads folder.

  2. Applications

    2. Drag it to Applications

    Drag PocketPals onto the Applications folder.

    • Open
    • Show in Finder
    • Get Info

    3. Right-click → Open, the first time

    Then click Open again. After that, open it normally.

Why the extra step? I'm a student, so my apps aren't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account). macOS asks you to confirm once that you trust an app from the internet. The app is exactly the one built from my source, and you can check the checksum below.

What's new

Version 1.2.1

22 November 2026

Bug fixes: continuous snow, proper snow piles with the plough, fixed text layout, Update Now button, and a big reliability pass

A quality release: no new features, just everything working the way it should.

Fixed

  • Snow no longer stops and starts. Coming back from a fullscreen app rebuilt all of Pocket Pals' windows, which threw away the snow (and, while a fullscreen app was showing, it tried to rebuild them every second). Falling snow is now its own simulation that keeps going through fullscreen apps, sleep, display changes and settings changes, eases up or down over about two seconds when you change how hard it snows, and is already falling when it first appears. Low battery pauses it below 20% and resumes above 25%, and a flickering weather forecast no longer switches it on and off.
  • The snow plough really piles up the snow. With the Dock along the bottom, the plough often stood on a surface that had no snow, so it didn't scrape anything. Now the blade scrapes the snow down to a thin dusting and pushes all of it into a bank in front of the blade, with natural sloping sides. The bank grows as you push it and stays put when you stop or turn round. A full blade spills clumps over the top, and banks tip off the end of the Dock. With the blade up you leave tyre tracks. Banks are part of the snow, shaded to match, and you can click one to make a snowball, a snowman or a fort from its snow. No snow is ever created or lost along the way.
  • The snow intensity control no longer glitches. The menus and Pet Home were being redrawn many times a second while the plough moved (and once a second otherwise), which made the Light / Medium / Blizzard control slide about. They now only update when something you can see changes.
  • Text fits. Reminder buttons no longer cut off long pet names, the Winter tab's boxes use the full width, and speech bubbles and name tags wrap neatly instead of growing very wide.
  • Walking off the end of the Dock no longer makes pals dip and "land" again (with a puff of snow) for no reason.
  • Snow landing on a tall snowbank no longer makes it smaller.
  • Old windows' memory is now freed when Pocket Pals rebuilds its windows (after display changes, waking and similar).
  • If your pets file is ever damaged, Pocket Pals now brings back your last good save instead of starting over (the damaged file is kept too).
  • Quitting with kill (or anything else that sends SIGTERM) now saves first.
  • Pocket Pals pauses while another user is using the Mac (fast user switching).

Added

  • Update Now in Settings › Updates: see what's new, then one click downloads the update, checks its signature, installs it and reopens Pocket Pals with your pals as they were. There's also Remind Me Later and Skip This Version, a progress bar with Cancel, and clear messages (with Download Manually) if anything goes wrong. Check for Updates… in the menus opens this.
  • Bring all pets back in the menu bar popover and the Help menu, and pals or items that end up off every screen come back by themselves.
  • If Pocket Pals ever closes unexpectedly, it says so kindly the next time it opens ("Your pets are safe") and offers to open a pre-filled problem report. Nothing is sent automatically.

Install: download PocketPals.dmg, drag PocketPals to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).

  • v1.2.021 Nov 2026Notes

    Pets stay on your desktop, perch on minimised windows, Winter Wonderland with snow plough, and menus above the Dock

    Added

    • Winter Wonderland. Turn on snow from ❄️ in the menu bar (with Light, Medium and Blizzard), or let Auto snow in your winter: June to August in the Southern Hemisphere, December to February in the Northern, worked out from your region (you can override it). With weather on, it also snows when it's really snowing outside, with real wind.
      • Snow falls in two layers of six-pointed flakes, behind your windows (or over everything, if you prefer), and scatters when you whoosh the pointer through it.
      • It settles on the Dock, on Dock icons and minimised windows, on your items, and if you like on window title bars and along the menu bar. Pals leave footprints and land with a puff; snow slides off windows you drag and tumbles off restored windows. Option-drag to wipe snow away. When snow is turned off it melts over about 30 seconds; Clear all snow is instant.
      • A snow plough you can drive. Click it, then drive with ← → or A D, Space lifts the blade and Shift goes faster. It pushes snow into piles (no snow is ever lost), with spray, an engine putter and a reversing beep. Click a pile to make a snowball, a snowman (bigger piles make bigger snowmen) or a snow fort, or flatten it. Pals ride along and chase it, and an optional auto-plough clears the Dock by itself.
      • Winter items: snowmen you can dress (hats, scarves, faces, buttons and arms) that slowly melt when the snow stops, throwable snowballs, a frozen pond pals slide on, a sled, a cosy fireplace and hot chocolate pals curl up beside, candy canes, icicles, big snowflakes and a wreath that hang from the menu bar. Right-click any item to resize (or scroll over it), duplicate or remove it, and Tidy up / Restore my winter scene in Pet Home's new Winter tab.
      • Christmas (Off, On, or Auto from 1 December to 6 January, separate from snow): a Christmas tree in three sizes with ornaments, a star or angel topper and twinkling lights, strings of fairy lights that hang like the real thing with five colour themes and five modes, presents your pals open, and an advent calendar with a little surprise behind every door.
      • Pals in winter: automatic scarves, beanies, earmuffs and boots, little puffs of breath, snow angels, catching snowflakes, digging, shaking off snow, sliding on ice, building tiny snowmen of their own, and curling up by the fire. Reindeer antlers, a Santa hat, earmuffs and boots are in the wardrobe.
      • Winter visitors: a robin flies by now and then, and a sleigh crosses the sky on Christmas Eve.
      • Snowball Fight, a seventh mini-game: aim and throw at snowman targets while your pals lob snowballs back at the pointer.
      • New sounds: wind, snow crunch, the plough's engine, scrape and beep, snowball throws, jingle bells and a music-box tune.
    • Perch on the Dock. Pals sit on Dock icons and minimised windows, choose perches by themselves (minimised windows are favourites), and react when a window is restored or the Dock hides. Drag a pal over a Dock item and it glows: let go to perch it there. Clicking the icon under a perched pal still goes straight to the Dock. Works with the Dock on the bottom, left or right. This reads where Dock items are with Accessibility permission (explained in onboarding and Settings, with an Open System Settings button); everything else works without it.
    • Where pets appear (Settings › Basic): All desktops (the default) or Only this desktop, with a Move pets to this desktop button.
    • Help › Getting Started now includes the Dock perches and a short winter tour.

    Changed

    • Pets now live above the Dock, so pals perched on it, their speech bubbles and name tags are never hidden behind it. They stay below menus, notifications and the screen saver.
    • The round menu, item menus, reminder bubbles, guided breaks and the game panel now open above the Dock. Near the Dock the round menu opens as an upward half circle, right under the menu bar it opens downwards, and near the sides it shifts inwards so every button is on screen. It pops open, and you can use the arrow keys and Return, or Escape (or a click anywhere else) to close it.
    • Speech bubbles and name tags stay inside the screen: under the menu bar they move below the pal, and at the edges they shift inwards with the tail still pointing at your pal.

    Fixed

    • Pals no longer appear inside fullscreen apps. The menu, reminder and game panels were allowed into fullscreen Spaces, and with "Over fullscreen apps" switched off the pets' own windows were too. Nothing joins a fullscreen Space now, and each display's pets hide instantly (and pause) while it shows a fullscreen app, then peek back when you return to a desktop. Works with multiple displays, "Displays have separate Spaces" and Stage Manager.
    • The round menu could open behind the Dock when you right-clicked a pal standing on it.
    • Weather outfits: the raincoat and scarf were never actually worn. Pals now wear them (in empty outfit slots), and splash through puddles when it rains.

    Install: download PocketPals.dmg, drag PocketPals to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).

  • v1.1.020 Nov 2026Notes

    30+ new animals, a simpler Pet Home, toys and beds, mini-games, pets that grow up, and much more

    Added

    • 37 new animals, each with its own colours, idle animations, behaviours and sound, all drawn in code in the same soft style:
      • Aussie Mates: quokka (big smile when petted), wombat (naps bottom-first in a burrow it digs in the Dock), koala (hugs window edges and dozes), kangaroo (bounds along the Dock; a joey peeks out of an adult's pouch), platypus, echidna, sugar glider (glides between windows, hangs upside down from the menu bar), kookaburra (laughs at dawn and dusk), cockatoo (crest dance to music) and blue-tongue lizard.
      • Cute & Cosy: otter (floats on its back and holds hands with another otter), sloth, capybara (other pals sit on its head), raccoon, squirrel, guinea pig, chinchilla, ferret, mouse, lamb, piglet, pony, fawn, seal pup, polar bear cub and panda.
      • Tiny Friends: snail (a shimmering trail), bee (visits flowers and does a waggle dance), butterfly, ladybug, octopus (changes colour with its mood), jellyfish (glows in Dark Mode) and gecko.
      • Special, unlockable animals: Unicorn (friendship level 10), Phoenix (a 14-day break streak), Little Robot (adopt 10 different animals) and Ghost Cat (visits every October; keep it with the Halloween achievement). Locked animals show as silhouettes with how to unlock them.
      • New looks for old friends: beagle, poodle and dalmatian-style dogs; tortoiseshell, cream and tuxedo cats; lionhead and angora bunnies; lovebird and canary; two more red pandas (and a new "stand up tall" surprise); Emerald, Sapphire, Ruby and Gold baby dragons (who learn to breathe sparkles).
    • Pet Home, one friendly window with big tabs: My Pals, Adopt, Wardrobe, Toys & Home, Games, Reminders and Settings. Open it from the menu bar, by opening the app again, or by holding ⌃⌥P for a second.
    • Right-click a pal (or Control-click) for a round menu: Feed, Play, Pet, Trick, Sleep, Wardrobe, Hide and Pet Home.
    • Toys, beds and a desktop home: beds, food and water bowls, toys (ball, yarn, scratching post, carrot, wheel, perch, flowers, puddle, pool) and decorations (plant, a lamp that glows at night, rug, seasonal pieces). Pals use them by themselves. Drag them onto your desktop, move them around, or tidy them all away at once. Unlock them with treats, earned by taking breaks, playing and petting (treats can never be bought).
    • Six mini-games on your desktop: Fetch, Hide & Seek, Treat Catch, Copy Me, Bubble Pop and Pet Race. Each takes a minute or two, keeps a best score and earns treats and friendship.
    • Pals grow up: new pals start as babies (smaller, clumsier, sleepier), become young after 3 days and grown-ups after 10, with a little party and a diary photo each time. You can keep any pal a baby forever.
    • Tricks you can practise: tricks unlock with friendship and get smoother with practice (new ones sometimes go a bit wobbly).
    • A daily diary of cute moments ("Biscuit caught 12 balls today!"), with little snapshots you can share as a picture.
    • Pet friendships: pals who spend time together become friends, best friends nap and play together more, and you'll see hearts between them in My Pals.
    • The Pet Hotel: saying goodbye now sends a pal to the hotel with everything they've learned. Welcome them back any time.
    • Guided breaks: your pal leads a short stretch routine, a 20-20-20 eye break with a countdown, or a breathing exercise where it puffs up and down with you.
    • Study with me: your pal puts on reading glasses and studies quietly for a focus session, then celebrates and plays during the break.
    • Reminder styles: Gentle (your pal sits beside your window), Normal or Persistent. Reminders are now written as plain sentences ("Remind me to stretch every 45 minutes"), and there's a weekly break report.
    • A friendlier first run: a waving welcome, an optional "Find your perfect pal" quiz, naming with name ideas, a short interactive tutorial that ticks itself off, and one simple question about break reminders. Replay it any time from Help.
    • Shortcuts and Spotlight: Feed my pets, Call a pal, Start a break, Hide all pets, Study with me, and Shy mode.
    • Never in the way: pals move aside if the pointer rests on them while you're clicking or typing, never sit on a window's buttons or toolbar, and (optionally) keep clear of the text cursor. Shy mode sends them to the corner, small and quiet. Double-tap Option to hide them all (optional).
    • Hide automatically during screen sharing, presentations, video calls and fullscreen apps (each its own switch, all on), then peek back afterwards.
    • Name tags with a mood face above each pal: always, on hover or never.
    • Optional extras, all off until you turn them on: weather outfits (raincoats, beanies and scarves, sunglasses, snow on the Dock; weather data by Open-Meteo.com), calendar awareness (good-luck wishes before exams and interviews, quieter pals on busy days), and celebrating finished downloads. Pals also fan your Mac when it runs hot and yawn late at night.
    • Special days for your region, such as Australia Day hats, as well as adoption anniversaries, your birthday, Christmas, Easter and Halloween.
    • Pocket Pals listens for Lectern's study timer and Pulse's CPU readings, if those apps announce them, to start Study with me or fan a hot Mac.
    • VoiceOver describes each pal ("Biscuit the Corgi-style Dog, happy, sitting on the Dock"), and Pocket Pals follows Reduce Motion (no screen-crossing zoomies) and Increase Contrast (stronger outlines).

    Changed

    • Adoption Centre and My Pals are now part of Pet Home, and the menu bar menu is a simple popover: your pals with Feed, Play and Call buttons, then Pet Home, Shy mode, Pause reminders and Hide all pets.
    • The menu bar icon shows your main pal's face, which changes with its mood, and a small dot when a pal needs something.
    • Settings are now split into Basic and Advanced, with a search box, an ⓘ on every option and "Restore defaults" for each section. "Pets can climb onto windows" became "Where pets can go" (Dock only, Dock and windows, or Everywhere).
    • Feeding is one click (your pal's favourite snack), with other foods a click away, instead of three menus deep.
    • "Put to sleep until I wake them" is a real setting now, rather than a year-long nap.
    • ⌃⌥P: tap to hide or show pals as before; hold it for a second to open Pet Home.
    • Uses less energy: lower frame rates when pals are idle or asleep, far fewer window checks, and textures that are drawn once and kept on disk.

    Fixed

    • If an overlay window ever goes missing, Pocket Pals makes a new one, and pals that end up off screen (for example after unplugging a display) walk back into view.

Questions

Report a problem
Will the pets get in my way?

No. Clicks go straight through to your apps unless you click on a pet, and you can hide them any time with Control-Option-P.

Can my pet get sick or run away?

Never. Pocket Pals is gentle by design: pets don't get sick, run away or make you feel guilty.
