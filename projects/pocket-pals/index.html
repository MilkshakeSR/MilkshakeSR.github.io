About Pocket Pals

Little animals that live on your screen. They walk along your Dock, nap on your windows, wake up when you come back and remind you to stretch, drink water and rest your eyes.

Every pal is drawn in code and has its own personality, and clicks go straight through to your apps unless you click a pet.

Screenshots

Features

  • 12 original animals, plus a secret Baby Dragon that hatches after 7 days together
  • Pals walk on your Dock, hop onto windows and ride along when you move them
  • Pet, play fetch, and teach tricks as your friendship grows
  • Gentle break reminders for stretching, water, eyes and posture, with optional Pomodoro
  • Reminders wait politely during calls, screen sharing and Focus
  • Photo Booth for cute portraits with frames and stickers
  • Light on your Mac: animation slows when pets idle and stops when the screen is locked

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later)
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later

How to install

On a Mac

  1. 1. Download and open

    Download PocketPals.dmg and double-click it in your Downloads folder. A window opens.

  2. 2. Drag it to Applications

    Drag Pocket Pals onto the Applications folder. Then you can eject the disk image.

  3. 3. The first time: right-click → Open

    In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Pocket Pals and choose Open, then click Open again. After that, open it normally.

    Why? Apple checks apps from the internet for a paid "notarisation" stamp. I'm a student, so my apps aren't notarised yet. That doesn't mean anything is wrong. macOS just asks you to confirm once that you trust where the app came from.

  4. No "Open" option? (macOS 15 Sequoia and later)

    Newer versions of macOS may only offer Done or Move to Bin. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Pocket Pals. You only need to do this once.

How updates work

Pocket Pals checks for updates automatically once a day. You can also choose Check for Updates… from the menu bar icon, or change this in Settings → Updates.

Privacy

Pocket Pals only looks at window positions and sizes so pets can stand on them. It never reads window contents, takes no screenshots and needs no Screen Recording permission. Your pets stay on your Mac. The only thing that goes online is the daily update check to GitHub. No analytics, tracking or accounts.

FAQ

Will the pets get in my way?

No. Clicks go straight through to your apps unless you click on a pet, and you can hide them any time with Control-Option-P.

Can my pet get sick or run away?

Never. Pocket Pals is gentle by design: pets don't get sick, run away or make you feel guilty.

Is Pocket Pals free?

Yes. Pocket Pals is completely free, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Is it safe to open an app that macOS warns me about?

The warning appears because Pocket Pals isn't notarised by Apple (that needs a paid membership), not because anything is wrong. Only download it from this site or its official GitHub page, and macOS will only ask you once.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Pocket Pals is built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later), so it won't run on older Intel Macs.

    Dance to music and follow the Dock

    Added

    • Hover over the Dock icons and your pets come over and follow the pointer along the top of the Dock, side by side. Pets up on a window or the menu bar hop or fly down to join in.

    • Pets never go below the top of the Dock any more. The floor beside the Dock is level with it, and you can't drag a pet underneath it either.

  3. 1.0.0

    First release

    Added

    • Twelve original, hand-coded animals: Dog (6 variants), Cat (6), Bunny (4), Bird (6, including an owl that's livelier at night), Hamster (3), Fox (2), Red Panda, Penguin, Frog, Axolotl, Hedgehog and a Baby Dragon that hatches after 7 days together.

    • Gentle care: happiness, hunger and energy, favourite foods and a friendship level that unlocks tricks and accessories. Pets never get sick or run away.
    • Break reminders (stretch, water, eye 20-20-20, posture, wrap-up time) presented by your pet, with Done, Snooze and Skip. They wait for calls, screen sharing, fullscreen apps and Focus, and restart after you've been away. Streaks, a weekly summary and an optional Pomodoro mode.
    • Adoption Centre with live previews, colour tweaks, accessories, names, personalities and sizes. A parachute arrival.
    • My Pals with stats, renaming, outfits, naps, a Photo Booth that saves to Pictures, and goodbyes with undo.
    • Menu bar menu with quick actions, global shortcuts (Control-Option-P to hide or show pets), Settings, onboarding and automatic updates.

    Install: download PocketPals.dmg, drag PocketPals to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).
