Lumen Drift: Twin Tides

A glowing 1–2 player swimming adventure for Mac and Windows.

Coming soon. Lumen Drift: Twin Tides isn't released yet. Follow new releases with the RSS feed or on GitHub.

Meet Lumen Drift: Twin Tides.

You play a Glimmer, a small glowing sea sprite. The ocean's great lights are fading: swim through dangerous water to relight every Beacon, alone or with a friend on the same keyboard.

Everything is procedural: shaders, particles and synthesised audio.

And there's more.

  • 1–2 player co-op on one keyboard or with controllers

  • Dash

    Dash, pulse and emote your way past the dangers of the deep

  • Procedural shaders

    Procedural shaders, particles and synthesised audio

  • Fully remappable controls and Photo Mode
