Meet Midnight Blackjack.

A real six-deck table with a visible cut card, a padded leather rail and gold printing on the felt. Cards arc out of the shoe, the dealer peeks and draws, and a Strategy Coach teaches basic strategy for your exact rules. Play money only.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. MidnightBlackjack.dmg

    1. Open the download

    Double-click MidnightBlackjack.dmg in your Downloads folder.

  2. Applications

    2. Drag it to Applications

    Drag MidnightBlackjack onto the Applications folder.

    • Open
    • Show in Finder
    • Get Info

    3. Right-click → Open, the first time

    Then click Open again. After that, open it normally.

Why the extra step? I'm a student, so my apps aren't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account). macOS asks you to confirm once that you trust an app from the internet. The app is exactly the one built from my source, and you can check the checksum below.

  1. MidnightBlackjack-Windows.zip

    1. Unzip it

    Right-click MidnightBlackjack-Windows.zip → Extract All…

  2. MidnightBlackjack.exe

    2. Run it

    Open the folder and double-click MidnightBlackjack.exe.

  3. Windows protected your PCMore infoRun anyway

    3. More info → Run anyway

    If SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Why does Windows warn me? SmartScreen warns about apps that haven't been downloaded many times yet or aren't signed with a paid certificate. It's a one-time check.

What's new

Version 1.0.0

15 November 2026

Added

  • A full blackjack table: 6-deck shoe with a visible cut card, dealer peek, insurance and even money, doubles (including after a split), splits up to four hands, late surrender, and 1–3 hands at once.
  • Five rooms from the Emerald Room (1K–1M) to the Infinity Lounge (no maximum), each with its own felt and chips, from 1K to the iridescent 1B diamond chip.
  • Starting bankrolls from 100K to 1B, or Unlimited mode. Free top-ups any time. Play money only.
  • Configurable rules (decks, H17, 6:5, double after split, re-split aces, surrender, no-hole-card, continuous shuffle) with the house edge for each, measured by simulation.
  • Optional Perfect Pairs and 21+3 side bets.
  • Strategy Coach: hints and mistake warnings from basic strategy generated for the exact rules, a colour-coded strategy chart, and accuracy tracking with your most common mistakes.
  • Card counting trainer: Hi-Lo running and true count, count checks and a speed drill.
  • A three-hand guided tutorial, 31 achievements, a daily challenge, a win-streak flame, statistics with a balance chart, and the last 100 hands with replays.
  • Original cards, chips, art, sound effects and lounge-jazz music; optional dealer voice.
  • Fullscreen on any display, keyboard and controller support, accessibility options and an in-game update checker.

Questions

Report a problem
Is this real gambling?

No. Midnight Blackjack uses play money only. Chips have no cash value and can't be bought or withdrawn. There are no ads, purchases or links to gambling sites.

Why does my Mac say the app can't be opened?

The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it, right-click it in Applications and choose Open, then click Open again. You only need to do this once.

Does it run on Windows?

Yes. Download the Windows zip, unzip it anywhere and run the .exe. If SmartScreen warns you, click More info → Run anyway.
