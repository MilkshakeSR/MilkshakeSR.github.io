Hello!

I'm a student developer who loves making friendly, polished software for the Mac. Most of what I build starts as something I wanted for myself: a way to keep an eye on my Mac's performance, plan a trip on a student budget, or have a little animal keep me company while I study.

I write native apps in Swift and SwiftUI and make games in Godot. Every app I release is free, keeps your data on your own computer and updates itself.
