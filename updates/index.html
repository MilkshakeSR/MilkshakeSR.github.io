  1. Midnight Roulette 1.0.1

    10 Nov 2026
    What's new in Midnight Roulette 1.0.1

    Changed

    • The eight diamond deflectors on the ball track are larger polished brass and the ball strikes one on most spins.
    • Taller, thicker frets between the pockets, and a livelier rattle: the ball hops pocket to pocket, can be knocked back a pocket or overshoot the result and bounce back. Results are still drawn fairly when you press Spin.

  2. Pocket Pals 1.0.3

    10 Nov 2026
    What's new in Pocket Pals 1.0.3

    Pets stay on their desktop

    Fixed

    • Pets really do stay on their desktop now. They could turn up on another Space or over a fullscreen app after your Mac woke from sleep, a display changed, or you hid and showed them. Pocket Pals now waits until you're back on the pets' desktop before redrawing anything, and hiding pets fades them out in place instead of moving their window.

    Install: download PocketPals.dmg, drag PocketPals to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).

  3. Pocket Pals 1.0.2

    9 Nov 2026
    What's new in Pocket Pals 1.0.2

    Dance to music and follow the Dock

    Added

    • Hover over the Dock icons and your pets come over and follow the pointer along the top of the Dock, side by side. Pets up on a window or the menu bar hop or fly down to join in.

    Changed

    • Pets never go below the top of the Dock any more. The floor beside the Dock is level with it, and you can't drag a pet underneath it either.
    • Pets dance the whole time Music or Spotify is playing, and stop when it pauses. This is now on by default. Pocket Pals listens for the play and pause announcements those apps make, so it reacts straight away and doesn't need any permission.

    Install: download PocketPals.dmg, drag PocketPals to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).

  4. Midnight Roulette 1.0.0

    9 Nov 2026
    What's new in Midnight Roulette 1.0.0

    Added

    • European single-zero roulette with every standard bet: straight up, splits (including 0-1, 0-2, 0-3), streets, trios, corners, First Four, six lines, columns, dozens and the six even-money bets.
    • A smooth 3D wheel built entirely from procedural meshes and shaders, with a ball path that is planned to land in the result drawn when you press Spin. Normal (~10 s), fast (~4 s) and instant modes, plus an optional ball-following camera.
    • A vertical betting table with exact click zones for every bet, hover previews, drag-to-move stacks, undo, rebet, double and saved bet layouts ("My Bets").
    • Racetrack call bets: Voisins du Zéro, Tiers du Cylindre, Orphelins, Jeu Zéro and neighbours bets.
    • Dolly, rake and payout animations, and tasteful celebrations for straight-up, big and huge wins.
    • Results history (last 500 spins), statistics for the last 100 spins and all time, hot/cold numbers and an optional heat overlay.
    • Configurable table limits, La Partage, extra $500/$1,000 chips, casino-mode betting timer and swap-panels layout.
    • Original synthesised sound effects, lounge music and casino ambience; optional dealer voice using the computer's text-to-speech.
    • Accessibility: colour-blind markers, text size 80–150%, reduce motion, full keyboard and controller support.
    • Wellbeing reminders and a clear play-money-only notice.
    • Auto-save after every bet and spin (atomic writes) with safe recovery if the game closes mid-spin.
    • In-game update checker that looks for new releases on GitHub.

  6. Pocket Pals 1.0.0

    9 Nov 2026
    What's new in Pocket Pals 1.0.0

    First release

    Added

    • Twelve original, hand-coded animals: Dog (6 variants), Cat (6), Bunny (4), Bird (6, including an owl that's livelier at night), Hamster (3), Fox (2), Red Panda, Penguin, Frog, Axolotl, Hedgehog and a Baby Dragon that hatches after 7 days together.
    • Pets live on your screen: they walk along the top of the Dock (or the screen bottom when the Dock hides), climb onto windows, ride along when you move them, tumble off when you drag too fast and peek out when another window covers them. Birds perch on a swing under the menu bar.
    • Seven expressions, a skeletal animation system and species tricks: fetch, zoomies, kneading, yarn-pushing, binkies, singing, hamster wheel, fox pounce, red panda climbing, penguin belly slides, frog fly-catching, axolotl bubbles, hedgehog curls and dragon smoke puffs.
    • Petting (wiggle the pointer slowly), clicking, double-click tricks, pick up and throw, cursor chasing. Pets play, nuzzle, scuffle and nap together.
    • Naps with "z z z" when you're away for 3 minutes, and a happy greeting when you're back. Nightcaps after 10pm, seasonal outfits, music dancing, a low-battery bubble and birthday confetti.
    • Gentle care: happiness, hunger and energy, favourite foods and a friendship level that unlocks tricks and accessories. Pets never get sick or run away.
    • Break reminders (stretch, water, eye 20-20-20, posture, wrap-up time) presented by your pet, with Done, Snooze and Skip. They wait for calls, screen sharing, fullscreen apps and Focus, and restart after you've been away. Streaks, a weekly summary and an optional Pomodoro mode.
    • Adoption Centre with live previews, colour tweaks, accessories, names, personalities and sizes. A parachute arrival.
    • My Pals with stats, renaming, outfits, naps, a Photo Booth that saves to Pictures, and goodbyes with undo.
    • Menu bar menu with quick actions, global shortcuts (Control-Option-P to hide or show pets), Settings, onboarding and automatic updates.

    Install: download PocketPals.dmg, drag PocketPals to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).

  7. Opal Outback 1.2.0

    8 Nov 2026
    What's new in Opal Outback 1.2.0

    Features happen more often and little wins look bigger

    Changed

    • Features happen more often: Free Games about 1 in 92 spins (was 1 in 145), Opal Lock about 1 in 80 (was 1 in 110) and the Stampede about 1 in 64 (was 1 in 81). Average feature prizes are a little smaller so the overall return stays about 96.3%.

    Fixed

    • A script error when the window lost focus while the game was still starting up.

    Added

    • Bigger little wins: each winning symbol flashes with a glowing ring, sparkles and a coin pop, the frame glow surges, the amount floats up over the reels and the WIN meter pulses.

    Downloads: OpalOutback.dmg (Mac, Apple Silicon) · OpalOutback-Windows.zip (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

    Play money only. Credits have no cash value and can't be bought or withdrawn.

  8. Opal Outback 1.1.0

    8 Nov 2026
    What's new in Opal Outback 1.1.0

    Reels stop in order 1 to 5, 3 Moons award 6 free games, and badges show what triggers each mini game

    Changed

    • Reels now always stop one at a time from left to right (reel 1 to reel 5), including when you press Spin to stop early.
    • Moonlight Free Games: 3 Desert Moons now award 6 free games (4 = 9, 5 = 12); Wild multipliers land x3 and x5 more often, so the overall return is unchanged (about 96.4%).

    Added

    • Requirement badges beside the reels showing what triggers each mini game, with the symbol icons (3 Moons = 6 Free Games, 6 Opals = Opal Lock).

    Downloads: OpalOutback.dmg (Mac, Apple Silicon) · OpalOutback-Windows.zip (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

    Play money only. Credits have no cash value and can't be bought or withdrawn.

  10. Driftscape 1.0.0

    8 Nov 2026
    What's new in Driftscape 1.0.0

    First release

    Added

    • Live wallpapers on every display and every Space, drawn behind your desktop icons with Metal, including behind the menu bar and notch.
    • 38 original, procedurally generated wallpapers in six collections: Water, Rain & Windows, Space, Nature, City & Cosy and Abstract.
    • Real time of day: the sun's position for your location drives sunrise colours, golden hour, dusk and starry nights, with the moon in its real phase.
    • Real weather from Open-Meteo every 30 minutes: clouds, rain, snow, fog, wind and thunderstorms fade in smoothly (rain takes two minutes to arrive). A weather preview menu lets you try Clear, Cloudy, Rain, Storm, Snow and Fog.
    • Optional music reactivity (off by default): wallpapers breathe gently with what your Mac is playing, using system audio, screen-capture audio or an input device. Sound is analysed live and never recorded.
    • Library with live hover previews, collections, search and favourites.
    • Studio with a large live preview, automatic controls for every setting, time/weather/music toggles, Randomize, Reset, undo and redo, and Save as Preset.
    • Schedules by time of day (dawn, sunrise, day, golden hour, sunset, night or set times) or a shuffle playlist, with crossfades.
    • A different wallpaper for each display.
    • Export a 5K still (PNG) or a seamless 10-second loop (MP4).
    • Lock screen and Mission Control kept in step with a still frame every 15 minutes, with your own wallpaper restored when you quit.
    • A Driftscape screen saver, installed with one click from Settings.
    • Energy saving: 30 fps at 75% resolution by default, and a complete pause when the desktop is covered, the screen is locked or asleep, in Low Power Mode or (optionally) on battery. A live CPU and GPU readout in Settings.
    • Menu bar menu, onboarding, Settings, automatic updates and a screen saver.

    Install: download Driftscape.dmg, drag Driftscape to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).

  11. Wanderfund 1.0.0

    7 Nov 2026
    What's new in Wanderfund 1.0.0

    First release

    Added

    • Trip planner with a friendly five-step wizard: city search with autocomplete, multi-city stops, fixed or flexible dates, travellers, travel style and accommodation.
    • Budget engine with a bundled dataset of 181 destinations (estimates at three travel styles), seasonal pricing, distance-based flight estimates, insurance, eSIM, shopping and an emergency buffer, all converted to your home currency.
    • Smart suggestions (hostel nights, shoulder season, food budget) and a Tight / Realistic / Comfortable reality check on every budget.
    • Date Finder: a 12-month value calendar and the top 5 date windows from your home airport, using seasons, NSW school holidays, public holidays, weather history, day of week and booking timing. Optional live flight prices with your own SerpApi or Duffel key, plus daily price watches.
    • Exchange: live ECB rates via Frankfurter, a converter, 90-day charts compared with the average, rate alerts and a travel money planner.
    • Study Exchange mode with a monthly budget, income and funding (including OS-HELP), shortfall or surplus, and a semester timeline.
    • Savings goals with progress rings, weekly/fortnightly/monthly targets, projections, a "what if" slider, reminders and milestone confetti.
    • Expenses in any currency with the day's rate stored, receipt scanning, cost splitting and settle up, a daily budget tracker and bank CSV import.
    • Reports with interactive charts, PDF trip reports and CSV export.
    • Day-by-day itinerary with maps, and packing, documents and before-you-go checklists (with passport expiry warnings).
    • Optional "Ask Wanderfund" assistant using your own Claude API key.
    • Dashboard with countdown, savings, payments due, rates, alerts and a world map; a menu bar helper; onboarding; JSON backups; and automatic updates.

    Install: download Wanderfund.dmg, drag Wanderfund to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time (the app isn't notarized).
